## Solutions by use case

Each page follows the same order: hero, the leak, how Shiplog fixes it, example campaign, CTA.

### Activation

H1: Turn signups into users.

Sub: Most signups never reach first value. Shiplog finds where each one got stuck and gets them moving, in app and by email.

The leak: You paid to acquire every signup. The ones who never activate are the most expensive customers you'll never have.

How Shiplog fixes it:

- **See the blocker.** Shiplog spots the exact step each user stopped at.
- **Remove it.** The next message is about that step, not a generic welcome series.
- **Escalate when it matters.** High value signups who stay stuck get flagged to your team.

Example: A fintech user started verification but never made a first transaction. Shiplog sent a short guide to the one missing document, then a reminder on day three.

CTA: Find your activation leaks. **Get your audit**

### Adoption

H1: Get customers using what they pay for.

Sub: Customers who use more of your product stay longer. Shiplog guides each account to the next feature that matters to them.

The leak: You ship features every week. Most customers never find them.

How Shiplog fixes it:

- **Match features to people.** Each user sees the feature that fits how they already work.
- **Time it right.** Tips appear when the feature is useful, not on a schedule.
- **Launch to the right users.** New releases reach the customers who asked for them.

Example: Users who export reports every week get a tip on scheduled exports the next time they export.

CTA: **Book a demo**

### Expansion

H1: Find the upgrades already in your base.

Sub: Accounts hitting their limits are ready to grow. Shiplog spots them and makes the right offer to the right person.

The leak: Upgrade revenue sits in your customer base while sales chases new logos.

How Shiplog fixes it:

- **Spot the signal.** Seat limits, usage caps and heavy feature use, tracked per account.
- **Reach the buyer.** The offer goes to the admin or budget holder, with their own usage numbers.
- **Close the loop.** Upgrades and replies flow back to your CRM.

Proof: 10 plan upgrades for one AI SaaS customer.

CTA: **Book a demo**

### Churn prevention

H1: Catch churn before renewal does.

Sub: Usage drops weeks before a cancellation. Shiplog notices, acts, and loops in your CS team when it matters.

The leak: Most churn is decided long before the renewal call. By then, it's too late to fix.

How Shiplog fixes it:

- **Watch every account.** Not just the top 50 your CSMs can track.
- **Act early.** A personal check in or a useful tip the week usage starts to drop.
- **Bring in humans.** High value accounts at risk get an alert to their owner.

Example: An account's weekly logins fell by half. Shiplog sent a check in from the CSM's name, then alerted the CSM when there was no reply.

CTA: **Book a demo**

### Reactivation and win back

H1: Bring quiet customers back.

Sub: Dormant users already know your product. Shiplog reminds them why they signed up, with something new to see.

The leak: Inactive users and churned customers are your cheapest growth, and most teams never email them again.

How Shiplog fixes it:

- **Find the right reason.** What changed since they left that matters to them.
- **Pick the right moment.** Seasonal patterns, product launches and their own history.
- **Stop when it's time.** No endless win back sequences.

Example: Users inactive for 30 days get one email about the single new feature closest to how they used the product.
